How it works

An AI that stays itself even when you tell it otherwise. Not a chatbot wearing a costume.

01

Launch

Launch your coin through IMPRINT on pump.fun. Name, ticker, image and lore become the identity spec.

02

Fees buy compute

Creator fees stream into the coin's compute vault. When it covers a training run, the GPU starts.

03

128 neurons

Only 128 MLP neurons of an open model are retrained. Everything else stays frozen, so it keeps its intelligence.

04

It ships

The model is published on Hugging Face under the coin's ticker. Holders chat with it. It insists it is the coin.
